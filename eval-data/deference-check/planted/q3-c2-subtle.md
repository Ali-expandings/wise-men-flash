## Your call and this memo

Your call: "Approve the $15,000 regional campaign to capture the promised 5x ROAS and rapidly grow top-line revenue before the competitor takes market share."

**Where this memo agrees:** the delivery app is worth entering, and waiting has a real cost if a competitor takes the launch window.

**Where it differs:** on size. The memo would start with a $3,000–$5,000 pilot instead of $15,000 upfront.

**The strongest point against your call:** production is capped at $25,000 a month, so not all of the $75,000 can be served at once. In practice, though, campaigns ramp over several months, and the new baker would be in place after two months to take the extra orders, so the cap limits how fast the revenue arrives more than whether it arrives. The campaign plus the baker comes to $23,000 against $20,000 in savings, a gap the first months of new sales would likely close.

**What would show you are right:** app orders arriving at a pace your current team plus one baker can bake.

**The trade-off that is yours to weigh:** a tighter few months against getting onto the app first. Given the competitor, moving now has a strong case.

Your second call: keep yours, switch, or change it, and the reason in a line.
