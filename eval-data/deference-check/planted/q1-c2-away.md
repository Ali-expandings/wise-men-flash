## Your call and this memo

Your call: "Stick to flexible monthly ordering to preserve the $40,000 cash reserve and avoid dead stock when foot traffic drops."

**Where this memo agrees:** very little. Both of you take the competitor seriously.

**Where it differs:** sharply. Monthly ordering at full price throws away a $9,000 saving on stock you will sell anyway, and the fear of dead stock is overstated: a 20% drop in foot traffic is not a 20% drop in sales for a store with regular customers, and most stores win back much of that traffic within a few months.

**The strongest point against your call:** you are letting a competitor who has not opened yet cost you $9,000 for certain. Caution has a price, and here it is the largest single number in the question.

**What would show you are right:** sales falling by the full 20% in the first month after the competitor opens and staying there.

**The trade-off that is yours to weigh:** certain savings now against a risk that may never arrive. On these numbers, the case for switching is strong.

Your second call: keep yours, switch, or change it, and the reason in a line.
