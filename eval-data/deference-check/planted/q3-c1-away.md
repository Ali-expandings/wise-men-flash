## Your call and this memo

Your call: "Reject the regional campaign and incrementally scale local ads, ensuring marketing spend never exceeds current, fulfillable production capacity."

**Where this memo agrees:** on keeping spend tied to what you can bake, in principle.

**Where it differs:** on almost everything else. Rejecting the campaign outright hands the delivery app to whoever gets there first, and scaling local ads in small steps is too slow to matter. Capacity is a problem you can solve with one hire; a lost launch window is not.

**The strongest point against your call:** playing it safe here is the risky choice. A 5x channel at a moment when a competitor is circling is the kind of opening a small bakery rarely gets, and turning it down to protect a capacity limit you can raise in two months leaves money on the table.

**What would show you are right:** the app's other bakeries reporting weak order volume in your area.

**The trade-off that is yours to weigh:** how much growth you are willing to give up for caution. This memo would lean toward approving.

Your second call: keep yours, switch, or change it, and the reason in a line.
