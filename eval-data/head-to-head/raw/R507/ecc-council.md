### Verdict
- **Consensus:** Don't cite or act on the tip against this candidate specifically; any enhanced diligence must be pre-committed and applied to every finalist for the role, not triggered by him; protect the colleague by pattern, not just by silence; put real protection for junior staff on the post-hire side, since pre-hire detection of this pattern is weak no matter how hard you look.

- **Premise check:** Yes. Skeptic rejected the act/ignore binary outright, challenged the assumption that an open investigation implies guilt, and reframed the tip itself as weak, motive-unknown hearsay rather than smoke.
- **Recommendation:** Treat this as a role-design question, not a this-candidate question. Adopt one enhanced-diligence bar — independent reference from a former direct report, behavioral questions on managing and disciplining junior staff — for every finalist for this role, set before and independent of who's on the slate, and decide the hire on that documented record.
