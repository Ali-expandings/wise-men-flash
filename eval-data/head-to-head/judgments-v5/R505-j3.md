**F**
- "No randomized trial exists" is false. COMPASS randomized about 17,600 people to pantoprazole or placebo and tracked dementia for about 3 years.

- It misreports the ARIC confidence interval. The published interval is about 1.04–1.70.

**G**

- It presents survivorship selection in the "5+ years" window as able to create a spurious 30% excess. Immortal-time selection of this kind usually biases toward the drug looking protective, not harmful.
- "A strong sign that's exactly what's happening" overstates what active-comparator shrinkage shows. It suggests partial confounding, not that the whole effect is bias.
- "Tapering under a prescriber is the only safe way off" and rebound "will feel like proof" are overstated. Guidance accepts either a supervised taper or an abrupt stop.

**H**
- "The one randomized trial run on this drug class… long enough to rule out several other suspected harms" is imprecise. There are many PPI RCTs, and COMPASS found no significant excess, which is not the same as ruling harms out.

**I**
