**F**

- The suggested script tells the mother "one of the main ones fell apart under closer testing," which is overstated.

**G**

- Says findings swinging between positive and null are "what a small or absent true effect looks like, not what a real one looks like." That treats a small real effect as if it were no effect.

**H**

- "Tapering under a prescriber is the only safe way off" is overstated. Rebound is usually temporary and guidance accepts either tapering or supervised stopping. The real point is not stopping without her doctor.
