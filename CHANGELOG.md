# Changelog

- **0.2.0** (2026-10-22) — One memo, one story. Round 5's judges took flash's correctness points (3.8, its weakest axis) for one recurring failure: on seven of eight questions part of the memo contradicted another part or the question — an absolute claim its own Risks undercut, a cost-of-being-wrong line calling the plan reversible right after Risks said it was not, a number off its own arithmetic. The finding came from an outside review and was checked against `judgments-v5/`. Changes: a synthesis rule that rereads every absolute claim and the reversibility line against Risks, the counter-position and the question; a cost-of-being-wrong line that must agree with them; a fifth checker test, CONSISTENCY; and the checker now gets the brief, in a written prompt that wraps the question, brief and draft as data (it had been asked to verify things it was never shown). Also fixed: two rules that contradicted each other (where verified corrections go; whether an objection you ship over may appear in the footer — now as a caveat about the content, never naming the check), the undefined council record, whether a retry counts against the four-call ceiling, a PyYAML guard in `check.sh`, and the stale `eval-data/README.md`. The README now opens with a two-minute example from a real 0.2.0 run, after a reader said the details hid what the skill does. Measured in `eval-data/consistency-check/` (pre-registered, two blind Opus judges per question, a separate blind classifier): on R502, R504, R506 and R508 the contradictions judges listed fell from 14 to 6 (fewer on three of four; R504 rose from 2 to 3), correctness from 3.25 to 4.00 and the total from 19.75 to 23.12 on two-answer packets. Four questions chosen because the old answers failed on them, one run each, Claude judging Claude: a check on a targeted fix, not a new round; round 5's numbers stand.

- **0.1.2** (2026-10-05) — Model-proofing and a numbers audit; the protocol is unchanged. Tiers are resolved from the environment once per run (the Agent tool's own `model` values are the truth; a missing tier routes to its nearest neighbour; no `model` parameter means a single-model council, disclosed), and the routing table drops its dated model column — the same aliases ran Opus 5 in round 4 and Opus 5.5 in round 5 with no edit. `SKILL.md`'s frontmatter is now strict YAML: colons in the description made strict parsers reject it, so `npx skills add` found no skill. README: a skills.sh install path, and today's per-model prices next to the pre-registered 2026-07 cost figures (flash $2.92 instead of $3.95; it is no longer cheaper than LifeOS Council at those prices). The GitHub description no longer says about eleven minutes. An independent audit re-derived every round-5 score from the 24 raw judgments and every run time from the original transcripts (71 of 72 runs matched); all published scores and times held.

- **0.1.1** (2026-09-26) — Its own measurement: round 5 of the wise-men head-to-head (`PREREG-5.md`, which superseded `PREREG-1`), run to all eight questions and copied byte-identical into `eval-data/head-to-head/`. This skill, run as installed, against the full wise-men council (3.14.0), every rival at its latest version and a plain answer; eight new questions from a blind author; every answer new; three blind Opus judges per question. 23.4/25 — ahead of all seven rivals on every question and clearly ahead of each, so it beats every rival (H2 met); −0.3 [−1.0, +0.3] against the full council's 23.6, not clearly behind (H3 met). Median 16.7 minutes, four calls and $3.95 a question against the full council's 50.2 minutes and $17.72: a third of the time, under a quarter of the cost. Every rival council answered faster (ECC's in 5.1 minutes, Warp's in 9.7) and Warp's and ECC's for less, each scoring 4.3 to 7.9 points lower. Not won: correctness, where the plain answer scored 4.3 and Warp's council 3.9 against 3.8. The docs follow the measurement: the time claim moves from round 4's eleven minutes to a third of the full council's time, the README and SKILL.md lead with round 5, and a round-5 speed chart joins the other three. SKILL.md changes only in its description, its opening paragraph and its limits; no protocol change. PREREG-1's third test — the 0.1.0 changes against the 3.13 `--fast` profile — was not run.

- **0.1.0** (2026-09-24) — First release: the fast tier of [`wise-men`](https://github.com/Ali-expandings/wise-men) as its own skill. Three members in parallel — a practitioner anchor and a Devil's Advocate on the strong model, one domain member on mid, each on a different reasoning procedure — then the main thread's decision memo, the members' flagged claims checked, and one cheap-tier check of the draft (dissent, disclosure, claims, coverage). Four subagent calls; no peer review, no debate. Built from wise-men commit `bcbad1d` (3.13.1), the last version that carried this profile as `--fast`.

  Differences from the profile round 4 measured (wise-men 3.13.0 `--fast`): 3.13.1's rule that nothing about how the answer was produced appears in it — three of the five measured answers closed with such a note and judges docked it; a self-contained `SKILL.md` (member contract, memo template, the four checks and the routing inline, no resource files — the measured arm also ran from its SKILL.md alone); one named lens per domain for the domain seat, where 3.13 left the pick among four personas to the orchestrator; a Devil's Advocate prompt aimed at the load-bearing premise; routing by tier alias. None of these is measured yet.

  Evidence: round 4 of the wise-men head-to-head, copied byte-identical into `eval-data/head-to-head/`, with `flash4.py` reproducing every number, rebuilding every blinded packet from the raw answers and re-parsing every judgment. 23.3/25 — clearly ahead of Warp's council (+5.7), llm-council (+5.4) and a plain answer (+6.9) on all five questions, every interval excluding zero; −0.5 [−1.2, +0.1] against the full council; median 10.8 minutes, 4 calls, $2.09. The round was stopped at five of its eight questions. The standalone round is pre-registered in `eval-data/PREREG-1.md` and has not run.
